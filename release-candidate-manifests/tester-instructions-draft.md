# WHAT TO TEST — Beta Lab release-candidate pass

This is a draft for Nelluk's review. Do not send or ping testers until Nelluk
separately approves delivery.

Run `/whattotest` in the beta server. You only need to do **one** useful test:

1. Choose **Give me a 5-minute test** for a short read-only task; or choose
   **Start guided session** for Team/House and game-result testing.
2. For a guided session, choose **one** of Team & House, Win claim, Confirm
   result, or Undo result. The private panel gives the exact slash fields and
   expected result.
3. Return to the panel and choose **Refresh results** after the command.
4. Choose **Finish and clean up** when done, even if you stop after one task.
5. If anything is wrong or confusing, choose **Report problem**. The report
   automatically includes the session and game context.

Please report unclear instructions, permission failures, stuck interactions,
incorrect public/private responses, duplicate effects, or cleanup that leaves
games or temporary roles behind.
